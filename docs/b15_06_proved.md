## B15-06-C4: how a stable floor yields a family conclusion

Status: EXACT implication, conditional on the specified dimension and witness.
Let A_delta be the nested ambient highest-weight spaces on S57's chart and
A_inf their union; let K_X=I(Z_X)_tau. Then I(X)_delta corresponds to
A_delta intersect K_X. Consequently

    i_X(delta)<=i_X_inf<=a_inf-r_X,

where r_X is any valid stable evaluation rank floor. If r_det=a_inf, K_det=0
and every valid rung has i_det=0, so D=-i_pad<=0. No assertion that all finite
coordinate multiplicities already equal their stable values is needed.

For a deficient stable floor and a finite padded ideal floor L_delta,

    D_delta<=a_inf-r_det-L_delta.

Only once the full ambient space is present may stable coordinate rank be read
as the finite coordinate rank. In particular stable sampling alone cannot
certify a positive gap. A positive at one cell needs a global determinant
ideal floor q and padded rank r_pad with q+r_pad>a_delta.

Verifier: elementary linear algebra of the displayed filtration. Dependencies:
inherited S57 Proposition S and rational rank-floor validity in C3.
