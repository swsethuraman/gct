## 1. A canonical operator and its hypotheses

Let V=Q^r, A=Sym(Sym^n V), and A_d its coefficient-degree-d part. These
are functions on Sym^n(V*). Use ordinary coefficients c_alpha, weight alpha,
and the derivations

    E_ij c_alpha = (alpha_i+1)c_(alpha+e_i-e_j)  (i != j, alpha_j > 0),
    E_ii c_alpha = alpha_i c_alpha.

For absent alpha_j the first expression is zero. Put

    C = sum_(i,j) E_ij E_ji,
    kappa_r(lambda) = sum_(i=1)^r lambda_i(lambda_i+r+1-2i).

Claim: C commutes with GL(V), and acts on S_lambda V by kappa_r(lambda).
Indeed [E_ab,E_ij]=delta_bi E_aj-delta_ja E_ib, so the four terms in
[E_ab,C] cancel in pairs after summing. On a highest-weight vector the
diagonal terms give sum lambda_i^2; for i<j the paired off-diagonal terms
give lambda_i-lambda_j. This is the displayed eigenvalue. The usual
characteristic-zero complete reducibility of polynomial GL modules now
gives the assertion on every copy. This is a representation-theoretic
premise, not an inference from sampled polynomial values.

Let Lambda be a complete finite envelope for the constituent partitions
of A_N. All partitions of nN of length at most r suffice without any
plethysm calculation. Suppose kappa_r(nu) differs from kappa_r(lambda)
for every other lambda in Lambda. With S the set of distinct eigenvalues,
define the normalized operator

    P_nu = product_(s in S, s != kappa_r(nu))
                 (C-s)/(kappa_r(nu)-s).

It acts as identity on the whole nu-isotypic component and as zero on
every other constituent. In particular P_nu^2=P_nu. A scalar multiple
other than one would no longer have this normalization. When a Casimir
eigenvalue collides, this formula selects a combined eigenspace; it does
not distinguish the colliding partitions. The implementation rejects
such a request. Joint central operators or a separate normalized tensor
construction would then be required.

For d+k=N the promised map is

    B_nu : A_d tensor A_k -> (A_N)_nu,   B_nu(F tensor H)=P_nu(FH).

Multiplication and P_nu are equivariant, so B_nu is equivariant. All input
objects are polynomials. If sum t_i F_i=0 as a polynomial, then
sum t_i B_nu(F_i tensor H)=0. Fillings enter only through their complete
coefficient-polynomial expansions; no raw box adjunction is used.

Claim: for every homogeneous GL(V)-stable ideal I,

    B_nu(I_d tensor A_k) is contained in I_N intersect (A_N)_nu.

Multiplication first places the result in I_N. Differentiating GL-stability
shows E_ij I_N is contained in I_N; hence every polynomial in the E_ij,
including P_nu, preserves it. The map therefore descends to A/I and
commutes with equivariant quotient maps. For closed GL-stable cones
X contained in Y, A/I(Y) -> A/I(X) commutes with these operators. This
supplies the needed direction of functoriality, but does not imply that
a particular projected product is nonzero or injective.

The GL-stability hypothesis is essential. In A_2 for ternary quartics,

    P_(6,2,0)(c_400 c_220) = (4/7)c_400 c_220 -(3/14)c_310^2.

The input is in the principal ideal (c_400), while the output is not.
That ideal is not GL-stable. This is a degree-two exact counterexample
to an unrestricted ideal-preservation claim; our claim includes stability.

## 2. A nonzero three-row non-Cartan map

Specialize n=4, r=3. The coefficient ring has 15 variables; A_2 has
dimension 120, A_3 has dimension 680 and A_4 has dimension 3060.
The first source is the irreducible module E generated by

    q = 8 c_400 c_220 -3 c_310^2,

of highest weight (6,2,0). Its dimension is 60. The output weight is
(6,4,2), of coefficient degree 3 and irreducible dimension 27. Its ambient
multiplicity a is one. This differs from the Cartan weight (10,2,0).
These representation dimensions must not be confused with multiplicities.

The complete degree-three character is the sum of the nine distinct
Schur characters at

    (12), (10,2), (9,3), (8,4), (8,2,2), (7,4,1),
    (6,6), (6,4,2), (4,4,4).

The integer coefficient-monomial character and the Weyl alternant give
these multiplicities, and their dimensions sum to 680. Our projector
uses the stronger envelope of all partitions of 12 of length at most 3;
its distinct eigenvalues are

    48,54,60,64,72,78,84,88,96,102,108,120,124,144,168.

Only (6,4,2) has eigenvalue 64 in that envelope. In contrast (8,2,2)
and (6,6,0) both have eigenvalue 84 and the collision control rejects
selection of either using C alone.

For an explicit normalization, abbreviate

    (a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i,j,k,l,m,n,o)
      = (c_400,c_310,c_301,c_220,c_211,c_202,c_130,c_121,
         c_112,c_103,c_040,c_031,c_022,c_013,c_004).

    G = 24adm -36ael +144afk -18agi +6ah^2
        -9b^2m +27bcl +3bdi +3beh -27bfg
        -54c^2k -12cdh +18ceg +8d^2f -2de^2

satisfies E_12 G=E_23 G=0 and

    B_(6,4,2)(q tensor c_022) = G/50.

At (a,b,...,o)=(1,2,...,15), G=3466, so the image is 1733/25.
The orbit-generated image is therefore the whole irreducible 27-dimensional
module. Fresh complete weight-block matrices also give rank 27 for

the matrix has 15 monomial rows and 22 tensor-weight columns, rank one.
The full map commutes with all four simple raising/lowering generators;

An independent construction keeps the multiplier letter distinct and

## 4. A costed next channel with a short normalized tensor

Take lambda=(65,17,2^7), degree 24, and
nu=(68,17,3,2^6), degree 25. B13-06 includes it among the 31 horizontal
four-strip channels, with tensor multiplicity one. The scoped ledger
returns no padded-gap exclusion and the accepted transport overlay marks
it OPEN. These are inherited scope checks, not a new gap claim.

For any HW polynomial F of weight lambda define, with zero exponents in
all unshown coordinates,

    T(F) = F c_301 -(E_32 F)c_310/15 -(E_31 F)c_400/16
                         +(E_21 E_32 F)c_400/240.

The rightmost root acts first. This is the multiplication image of a
normalized HW tensor with coefficient of F tensor c_301 equal to one.
Writing a=lambda_1-lambda_2=48 and b=lambda_2-lambda_3=15, the coefficients
are 1, -1/b, -4/(a+b+1), 4/[b(a+b+1)]. E_23 cancels by
1+b*(-1/b)=0 and -4/(a+b+1)+b*4/[b(a+b+1)]=0. E_12 cancels by
4*(-1/b)-(-4/(a+b+1))+(a+1)*4/[b(a+b+1)]=0. All other simple raising
operators commute with the used lowering terms on F and vanish on the
displayed coefficient factors. Thus T(F) is HW of weight nu, or zero.
The formula preserves every GL-stable ideal for the same reason as above.
Multiplying by 240 gives an integral operator on integral F.

A source arithmetic circuit can carry five values at each node:
F, E_21 F, E_32 F, E_31 F and E_21 E_32 F. A multiplication node costs
at most 11 field multiplications and 6 additions; an addition node costs
5 additions. The final expression adds at most 7 multiplications and
3 additions. This costs construction, reduction and evaluation without
allocating a full Schur module or coefficient carrier. The counts and
bit-complexity qualification are in next_channel.json; no wall-time
estimate is asserted without a measured source circuit and input heights.

The sufficient next witness for one determinant product equation is a
complete rational source F with global determinant-ideal provenance and
an exact nonzero T(F) value. A zero value at selected points would not
prove T(F)=0. Even nonzero T(F) proves only an image dimension, not the
full determinant or padded ideal. If it gives i_det>=1, a sufficient
positive-gap witness would still require padded rank a, or stronger
separated bounds. Annihilating three known padded equations would say
nothing about the remaining padded ideal directions.

No geometric goal-cell evaluation, new ideal multiplicity bound, or new
gap exclusion is claimed. True independent padding remains z*per3 with
ten essential variables; it is not replaced by a nine-variable point family.
