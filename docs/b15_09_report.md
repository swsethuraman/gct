  pullback rank is 16 and its kernel is generated by the discriminant.

  gives a complete evaluation determinant 1. All 22032 source evaluations

  expansion. Both rank-16 minors have determinant 70368744177664.
- The finite discriminant is nonzero in the ambient coefficient ring and is
  the unique det^2-relative invariant in degree five. Its chart formula is
  F=2c^5 det M, which clears the denominators and proves the multiplicity
  claim. The 17-dimensional coefficient weight space is not a multiplicity
  of 17.
- Separate liveness checks gave a nonzero three-direction trace-Gram minor
  (value 2), a nonzero ambient discriminant (value 32), and a finite pencil
  with leading determinant 3, coefficient rank 4 and traceless Gram rank 3.
  An altered determinant sign, normalization, kernel coefficient and repeated
