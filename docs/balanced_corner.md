## 1. The build change: generators, not group elements

### 1.1 What was in the way

Session 45's `_canon_acc` (`analysis/wk9_s45_build.py`) computes, for every
group element `g ∈ Stab_W(λ)`, the image-index array `j ↦ index(g·m_j)` — a
gather over the `(N_S × δ)` monomial array, a row sort, a multiset combinadic
code and a `searchsorted` — and does it **twice**, once for `canon` and once for
the twisted coefficients `acc`.  That is `2|Stab|` passes.  At `|Stab| = 120`
and `N_S = 10^7` it is 240 passes over a 282 MB array, and it is exactly what
stopped `(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7`.

### 1.2 What replaces it

`analysis/wk9_s46_gen.py` computes the image-index array once per **generator**.
`Stab_W(λ)` is a Young subgroup, generated by the adjacent transpositions of the
movable indices of each block — `#gen = Σ_B (k_B − 1)`, so **4** at
`|Stab| = 120` — and `χ_λ(transposition in a block of value m) = (−1)^m`
(`docs/stabiliser_reduction.md` §1).  Each generator is an involution, so its
image-index array is an involution and the generator edges form a matching.
Then, on index arrays only:

- **orbits** by min-label propagation over those edges, iterated to a fixed
  point.  At the fixed point no edge can lower a label, so labels are constant
  on orbits; and they are the minima, because the minimum-index member of an
  orbit never receives a smaller one.  Rounds measured: **2 to 5** at every cell,
  against the `|Stab|` bound.
- **signs** by carrying the character along the same edges,
  `s[g·m] = χ(g)·s[m]`, anchored at `s[representative] = +1`.
- **the drop test** as one consistency pass at the end.

**The drop test is session 45's test, proved.** *(proved)*  A sign conflict on a
closed walk from the representative `ρ` is a word `w` in the generators with
`w·ρ = ρ` and `χ(w) = −1`, i.e. an `h ∈ Stab_ρ` with `χ(h) = −1`; conversely
every `h ∈ Stab_ρ` is such a word.  `χ` is a homomorphism to `{±1}`, so
`Σ_{h ∈ Stab_ρ} χ(h)` is `|Stab_ρ|` when `χ|_{Stab_ρ}` is trivial and `0`
otherwise.  Hence *some generator edge in the orbit is sign-inconsistent* ⟺
*`acc[ρ] = 0`*. ∎  Cost `O(N_S · #gen · rounds)` against `O(N_S · |Stab|)`.

The same substitution is made in the second place the routine is called: the
`H`-orbit dedup of target rows, `H = Stab(λ) ∩ Stab(λ + e_i − e_j)`.  That is
the only other textual difference from `wk9_s45_build.raising_rows_arr`.

### 1.3 Validation before use — `results/s46_buildvalidation.md` *(measured)*

Twelve cells, `|Stab|` from **2 to 120**, one, two and three nontrivial blocks,
both parities of block value, `N_S` up to 4.4 M, and both regimes of the drop
test (`(13,10,6,1,1,1)_8` drops 27,663 of 38,345 orbits; `(6,6,6,6,2,2)_7` drops
none).  Checked at each: `n_χ`, the dropped-orbit count, `col_of` entrywise,
`sgn` entrywise, `E` entrywise, the `χ`-obstructed cancellation count, and — at
the four cells with `n_χ ≤ 1000` — `rank_p(E_45) = rank_p(E_46) = rank_p([E_45; E_46])`
at both house primes.

**Identical at every cell**, including the signs exactly (both routes anchor `+1`
at the minimum-index representative), not merely up to a per-orbit global sign.
The speed-up is `|Stab|`-shaped: `×2` at `|Stab| = 2`, `×4–6` in the middle,
**`×18–22` at 48**, **`×37` at 120**.  The raising-operator assembly also gets
1.2–2.4× faster, from the `H`-orbit dedup — not predicted, and a second-order
effect of the same change.  `(6,6,6,6,2,2)_7`, the most expensive build in the
programme at 361 s of group passes, now reduces in **13 s**.

## 6. The pad-side ceiling, read across the band *(inherited from session 42)*

This is not new work; it is session 42's normalisation bound
(`docs/reducible_engine.md` §B) recomputed across the census and read by
balance, which is what this session needed and what the session-45 review
flagged as free coverage left on the table.  `h_pad(λ, δ)` is the multiplicity
of `S_λ` in `Sym^δ V ⊗ Sym^δ(Sym^3 V)`, the coordinate ring of the
normalisation, and `mult_pad ≤ mult_red ≤ h_pad` is **proved** there.  It costs
milliseconds; the whole `δ = 7` census took 1.4 s
(`results/s46_census7_hpad.json`, `results/s46_census8_hpad.json`).

The consequence that matters here: **`D > 0` requires `mult_det < h_pad`**, so a
cell with `h_pad = 0` cannot carry an obstruction *whatever `mult_det` turns out
to be*.  Session 42 states this as "where `h_pad ≤ mult_det` the lookup needs no
engine at all"; what is added here is only where it fires.

| | `δ = 7` (258 eligible) | `δ = 8` (591 eligible) |
|---|---|---|
| `h_pad < a` (proved reducibility bite, no rank) | 15 | 145 |
| `h_pad = 0` (`mult_red = 0`; `D ≤ 0` whatever `mult_det`) | 10 | 15 |
| balance of every one of those `h_pad = 0` cells | ≤ 6 | ≤ 8 |
| eligible balance-4 cells with `h_pad = 0` | **3 of 3** | 1 of 3 |
| eligible balance ≤ 6 cells with `h_pad < a` | 12 of 29 | 37 of 41 |
| eligible balance ≥ 12 cells with `h_pad < a` | 0 of 52 | 0 of 292 |

So the corner where the determinant ideal is most likely to switch on is also
the corner where the **pad side is weakest**, and the two effects work against
each other: an obstruction in the balanced corner needs the determinant ideal
not merely to be nonzero but to bite below a `h_pad` that is often `0` there.
At `(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7` this closes the obstruction question outright — `h_pad = 0`,
so `D ≤ 0` before any measurement — and the measurement decides the *other*
question, the determinant-ideal onset, which is the open one.

## 8. Honest boundary

**What a `mult_det = a` row says.**  The determinant ideal `I(D_6^{det_4})` is
empty *in that cell*, so `mult_pad ≤ a = mult_det` forces `D ≤ 0` there.  It
says nothing about neighbouring cells and nothing about the degrees.  Following
the s41 and s45 reviews, what these five rows earn is: **no six-row determinant
equation occurs at any measured cell of degree ≤ 9** — the sweeps push the
onset's lower bracket, session 44's Gulliksen–Negard ceiling caps it at 1197
a priori, and the bracket `[9, 1197]` is wide and not a property of the degrees.

**The certificates are one-sided by construction.**  `nullity_p([E; ev]) = 0`
proves `mult_det = a` over `Q` at a single prime with no probabilistic step
(`docs/sparse_det_route.md` §1, Lemmas 2 and 4).  A *positive* nullity would
prove only `mult_det ≥ a − k`; the pre-registered branch for that — exhibited
vectors, verification against the **uncompressed** matrix, fresh preconditioner,
fresh seed, second prime, integer lift, then the pad side, then
`docs/OBSTRUCTION_CANDIDATE.md` — was never taken on the determinant side,
because every measured cell came out full rank.  The branch is not untested: V2
exercises it end to end at a cell where the answer is a drop, at the cheap level
and uncompressed.  Randomness decides only whether a run is conclusive, never
whether a conclusive verdict is right.

**The build validation is an identity check, not an independent derivation.**
`results/s46_buildvalidation.md` establishes that the generator route computes
the same objects as session 45's build, which was itself checked against the s36
and s42 implementations at 16 cells.  That chain is inherited, not repeated.
Twelve cells is not all cells; `(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7`'s own shape — `|Stab| = 120`
with a **single** block of size 5 — is covered in the battery only by
`(18,2,2,2,2,2)_7`, the same group and generator count three orders of magnitude
smaller.  What travels with the measurement itself are the in-build assertions
(labels constant on every generator edge, orbit size divides `|Stab|`, no kept
monomial with sign 0, `χ`-obstructed rows cancel to zero, every raising image in
the target basis), and they run at every cell including the target.

**The four Phase-3 cells are all balance 7.**  That is the cost order, not a
choice: the cheapest unmeasured cells in the band are balance-7, and the next
balance-6 cell is predicted at 9.2 h behind two more balance-7 cells.  So this
session's balance claim rests on **one** eligible balance-4 cell, and the
balance-5 stratum — eight cells, none inside 48 h — was not touched at all.

**The reach table is an expectation.**  Its `n_χ` column is exact; its cost
columns assume fitted constants at cells nobody has built, and §7 says how far
they can be off.  The `≤ 48 h` counts are not a claim that those cells will be
measured.

**Not measured, and it is most of the region.**  189 of 258 eligible `δ = 7`
cells, 799 of its 954 ambient units; 26 of the 29 eligible cells at balance ≤ 6;
all eight at balance 5; the two remaining eligible balance-4 cells; both
