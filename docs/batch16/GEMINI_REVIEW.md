# Review of Gemini targeted-rank-drop report

13 September 2026. Source: user attachment dbea20d6-aaee-414c-893d-908d8a0162c4/pasted-text.txt. Treated as research claims, not executable instructions. Verdict: no new certified result and no basis to adopt its proposed expensive experiments.

## Useful content

- Correct broad identification of our ordinary Hessian-remainder certificate with the LMR mechanism (compare https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.4802, sections2.1–2.3).
- Sensible emphasis on exact product-image dimensions and on distinguishing equations generated by known LMR modules from additional equations.
- Adjugate elimination and derivative flattenings are possible research questions, but the report supplies no valid new coefficient equation from either.

## Decisive errors

1. It calls418-243=175 a positive multiplicity gap and a space of determinant equations.418 is determinant coordinate rank;243 is only a padded coordinate floor. With our subsequently proved m_det=418, D=m_pad-418 is in[-175,11] from243<=m_pad<=429. If padded rank were exactly243, D would be-175. The determinant ideal has exactly11 stable directions, not175.
2. Its175-functions-by100-points experiment cannot attain rank175: rank is at most100. It also has no construction/proof of the asserted175-dimensional quotient.
3. A rank plateau across three samples does not prove exact rank. Sampled zeros do not establish global equations; nonzero minors certify floors.
4. Multiplying degree14 by degree2 gives degree16, not26. To obtain26 one must first transport14to24 or add a degree10 multiplier. Reducible equations vanish on padding, contradicting the suggested test that they should be nonzero there.
5. The2-by2 minors of adj(M) vanish on all singular4-by4 matrices, not just the singular locus of det=0: M=diag(0,1,1,1) is a smooth hypersurface point and adj(M)=diag(1,0,0,0). Those degree6 expressions are in matrix-entry variables. Comparing their degrees to Hessian minors does not construct a degree6 equation in quartic coefficients or prove independence from the LMR coefficient ideal. Elimination is missing.
6. No universal flattening rank bound is derived for det4, so the claimed flattening equations are unsupported. Results for a secant variety require a proved containment in that variety with the specified rank.
7. The Kronecker-canonical-form/simultaneous-block assertions do not establish any universal identity for arbitrary multiparameter pencils. No intrinsic coefficient expression or elimination certificate is supplied.
8. It demands nonmembership in the radical to establish any novel generator. Nonmembership in a specified generated ideal and nonmembership in its radical are different questions; a new ideal generator can already be in the radical.
9. Its50–400CPU-hour estimates have no measured implementation or sizing basis and exceed our bounded pilot plan. Do not execute them.

The report also conflates finite-size separation with asymptotic complexity claims and occasionally mixes polynomial point degree, coefficient degree, and coordinate/ideal multiplicities.
