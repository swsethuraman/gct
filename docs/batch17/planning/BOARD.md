# Batch17 board — new determinant constraints before rank hunting

Status: SET FOR REVIEW; NOT DISPATCHED. 13 September 2026.

## Objective and decision

Find a representation-specific determinant coordinate upper bound B together with a credible actual-padding lower-bound construction capable of exceeding B. A globally certified separating equation is a distinct useful output, but is not called a multiplicity obstruction. No positive candidate is currently established.

The prerequisite screens exclude all six nearby Batch16 cells and find no symmetry-certificate survivor in the specified degree-seven slice; see SCREEN_REPORT.md. Do not launch more evaluation jobs in those six cells or expand that character slice by default. The degree-seven result excludes only that certificate, not every possible closure-based obstruction.

## Operating rules

- Retain gpt-6-astra / xhigh for every research task. Reuse the twelve existing B15 worktrees if the batch is subsequently launched; check actual task, process, runtime and per-worktree Git state before dispatch. No sessions are created by this board.
- At most two heavy jobs. Only 01 and 02 are initially eligible for heavy leases, and even these start with theory and a priced preflight. A lease is issued only after the concrete calculation's time and aggregate-memory bounds are recorded. No automatic transfer to another slot.
- Ordinary operation-specific approvals. Sandbox configuration, ownership and Git trust stay unchanged. No push, publication or automatic commit. Keep closed Batch15/16 deliveries unchanged; new outputs go in owned b17 paths.
- Initial pilots: one process, one BLAS thread, at most 60 seconds and 512 MiB through the inspected Job Object wrapper. A cap hit means uncomputed. Larger jobs require explicit integrator resource review within the two-job ceiling.
- Every claim records scope, normalization, provenance, exact certificate and fresh/inherited status. A sampled rank plateau is only a floor. A source ceiling is never a padding lower bound.

## Priorities and expected learning

First priority: 03 plus 01, locating the first genuinely unresolved row length for actual padding and seeking a geometric determinant loss there. Second: 02, a new closure-sensitive equation mechanism. 04/05 consolidate usable certified structure. 06 prepares a real lower-bound method. 07 is a tightly scoped speculative audit.

A possible further exclusion through four rows would narrow the active length range to 5–10; do not promote it to an accepted all-degree theorem until 03 is reviewed. Even then these are infinite degree families, not six finite problems. First-equation-degree failures cannot exclude later degrees, and equation presence on both sides never replaces comparing multiplicities.

Success: a positive exact multiplicity gap; or a new valid determinant bound plus a qualified padding candidate; or a proved structural exclusion that materially narrows the search. An improved explicit separator is useful but separately labeled. No asymptotic claim without an explicit growing (m,n) inequality beyond the relevant benchmark.

Launch gate: user authorizes launch, integrator checks actual session/runtime/Git state, pins source inputs and writes per-slot prompts/dispatch receipts. This board alone grants no worker dispatch or heavy lease.
