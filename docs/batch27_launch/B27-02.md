## The gate

Batch 26 rejected fully paired tableaux at n=4. The reason is that every such tableau is nonzero
on `Q²`, and `Q²` is a determinant (A26-03 with B26-10C). The accepted reopening condition asks for
at least one of:
- a column with no identical partner;
- a combination of tableaux with coefficients of mixed sign.

**You choose the candidate. No one nominates it.**

## Ladder

**2a — Escape paragraph (at the checkpoint).** Define one candidate precisely:
- content, shape, columns and normalization;
- if it is a combination, its exact coefficients.

Show that it satisfies the reopening condition. If you cannot write this by 45 minutes, close the
slot as a feasibility result.

**2b — Visibility.** Give an exact certificate that the candidate sees `Sym⁴(span(x₃,x₄,x₅))`.
For example, show that `t ↦ f(q + tB²)` is nonconstant at a stated `q`.

**2c — The two known determinants.** Compute `f(Q²)` and `f(D₄)`, where
`D₄ = (A+B/2)² = det K` is the pencil from B26-02.
- Both values must be **0** for the candidate to remain a possible determinant equation.
- Either value nonzero is a rejection: record it, and go back to 2a **once**. At most two
  candidates per slot.

Use HAND or COMPUTED, and replay any value you rely on.

**2d — Padding.** Run this only if 2c passes. Evaluate at an actual-padding point. Prefer B27-01's
`T*` if it has been committed on `b27-01` by then; otherwise use B26-02's witness `p₄`. A nonzero
value **together with** a universal determinant identity would be a separator. Separately,
describe what a proof of the identity would need.

## Registered outcomes

1. A candidate passes 2c and is nonzero at an actual-padding point, together with a precise
   determinant-side question.
2. Separator. This requires a proved universal identity; it cannot come from evaluations.
3. Rejection of each candidate tried, each with an exact nonzero determinant value.
4. No defensible candidate, with the obstruction stated.

Passing 2c is **not** a proof of membership in the ideal. Keep the achievement levels distinct.
