## Background

A25-10 stated the reopening condition, and it still governs. At least one of the following is
needed:

- **Whole-ring containment.** Show `ker Q_D ⊆ ker Q_P` for the delivered five-center maps.
- **A witness.** Give one `h` with `Q_D h = 0` and an explicit actual-padding `T′` with
  `h(P_T′) ≠ 0`, at a justified total cost.

Batch 26 showed that `T′` must break symmetry. It also showed that the five-center projection
always discards the five squarefree quartics.

The inputs are the A25-05 maps (`eb53b97c`), A26-01 (`7464a2bd`), A25-10 `NEXT_ACTION.md`
(`ab4f5271`), and B27-01's `T*` if it has been committed on `b27-01` by then.

## Ladder

**3a — A nonsymmetric witness.**
- Fix an explicit rational actual-padding substitution `T′` whose 3×3 block is not symmetric under
  any linear change of variables that A26-01 covers. Use B27-01's `T*` if it exists; otherwise
  choose your own.
- Certify that `T′` is not in A26-01's literal family, using COMPUTED or HAND.
- Compute its 65 retained five-center coefficients `π(P_T′)` exactly.

**3b — Projection test.**
- Is `π(P_T′)` in the image `π(D)` of an **explicit** determinant pencil? Search only among
  certified constructions: A26-01-type presentations after a change of basis, and Pfaffian-square
  pencils.
- A literal preimage is a rejection of `T′` for this ring.
- Record the attempt precisely, and do not claim non-membership in the closure `Y` from a failed
  search.

**3c — Smallest nontrivial `h`.**
- In the lowest coefficient degree `k` where `ker Q_D` on the 65 coordinates is nonzero, determine
  that kernel exactly. Choose a bounded, stated weight space, sized so the allowance can handle
  it.
- Evaluate a basis of the kernel at `π(P_T′)`.
- If any value is nonzero **and** membership in `ker Q_D` is proved exactly, not sampled, that is
  the reopening witness: record it as outcome 1.
- If every value is zero, record a scoped containment for that degree and weight space. This
  counts as progress, not failure.

**3d — Price the next step.** Give the cost of the next degree or weight space, as a priced
preregistration.
