# I₆ and Ψ — does the tensor's fundamental invariant contain the evaluation's Ψ?
Session 19 (2026-08-30). Math/symbolic only; no engine run.

Open problem (2) from the session-18 notes: Ψ is now characterised canonically,
but nothing says **why the evaluation should track it**. The concrete route: the
net's tensor has slabs (I, A, B), so the natural home is the invariant ring of
C³⊗C³⊗C³, whose lowest generator has degree 6.

# PART I — PRE-REGISTRATION (written and committed BEFORE the comparison was computed)

## What has been established at the time of writing

**(A) The machinery is validated on a case with a known answer (step 2).**
A degree-2n invariant of an n×n×n tensor is a complete ε-contraction of
T^{⊗2n}: 2n copies, 6n indices, each slot carrying 2n indices absorbed by two
ε_n's. A *pattern* is a triple (P₁,P₂,P₃) of partitions of the 2n copies into
two n-blocks, one per slot. For n = 2 (degree 4) this machinery was run against
two independently cross-checked references —

* Ref A: the explicit 12-term Cayley polynomial;
* Ref B: disc of the binary quadratic det(x·S₀ + y·S₁) over the two slabs;

Ref A ≡ Ref B was verified symbolically first, so the reference is itself
cross-validated. Then, over the 27 patterns:

* rank of the pattern-evaluation matrix = **1** (the degree-4 invariant space is
  a line, as it must be);
* pattern ratios to Cayley: exactly {0, +2, −2};
* **symbolic identity** on the generic 2×2×2 tensor (8 indeterminates):
  contraction(((01)(23),(01)(23),(02)(13))) = **−2 · Det_Cayley**, exact;
* anchors: rank-1 ⟹ 0 (two different rank-1 tensors); GHZ e₀₀₀+e₁₁₁ ⟹ **1**;
  W-state e₀₀₁+e₀₁₀+e₁₀₀ (rank 3, tangent locus) ⟹ **0**; zero tensor ⟹ 0;
  generic rank-2 T = u⊗v⊗w + u′⊗v′⊗w′ ⟹ **9,572,836 =
  (det(u,u′)det(v,v′)det(w,w′))² = 3094²** — exact, a fifth independent anchor.

The anchor is exact. Machinery accepted.

**(B) Degree 6, 3×3×3 (step 4).** The S₆ relabelling reduction is *proved* (all
six copies carry the same T, S₆ acts transitively on the 10 partitions, so every
pattern is ± one with P₁ = ((0,1,2),(3,4,5))), leaving 100 patterns. A fast
two-stage exact evaluator was written and cross-checked against the naive
18-index contraction on 5 patterns (3 nonzero, 2 zero) — agreement exact.
Result over 60 random integer tensors (entries in [−40,40]):

* **rank of the 60 × 100 pattern-evaluation matrix = 1**;
* 36 of the 100 patterns are nonvanishing, and every one is **±1** times a fixed
  one; the other 64 vanish identically.

**Re-derived vanishing rule (step 3's caution honoured).** *Transposition
lemma.* Let τ = (a b) swap two copies. All copies carry the same T, so the
value of (P₁,P₂,P₃) equals that of (τP₁,τP₂,τP₃) up to the sign of restoring
canonical block order. A 3-element block cannot be exchanged with its
complement by a transposition, so τ fixes a partition **iff** a,b lie in the
same block, and then it transposes two arguments of that slot's ε — sign −1.
Hence if a,b share a block in **all three** slots, the pattern is fixed by τ
with sign (−1)³ = −1, so I = 0. **PROVED.** Machine check: this predicts
exactly 64 zeros, and exactly those 64 are observed (the converse — that no
other pattern vanishes — is *observed at 20 random points, not proved*).
This lemma uses only identical copies and ε-antisymmetry. It is **not** the
census law (two legs of one copy of a symmetric cubic into one ε, which relies
on symmetry of a second derivative); that argument does **not** apply here,
since T's three slots live in different factors. Nothing was transported.

**Literature (step 4's citation requirement).** Vinberg's θ-group analysis of
the ℤ/3-grading of E₆ gives: *the algebra of SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ) invariants of
3×3×3 arrays is **freely** generated by homogeneous polynomials of degrees 6, 9
and 12.* Confirmed from Bremner–Hu–Oeding, *The 3×3×3 hyperdeterminant as a
polynomial in the fundamental invariants for SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ)*, Math.
Comput. Sci. **8** (2014) 147–156 (arXiv:1310.3257), which credits É. B. Vinberg
(1976) for exactly this statement, and cites Nurmiev (2000) for the normal
forms and Bremner–Hu, *Fundamental invariants for the action of
SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ)×SL₃(ℂ) on 3×3×3 arrays*, Math. Comp. **82** (2013), for the
explicit generators (I₆ there attributed to Aronhold, 1152 terms). Freeness
forces **dim (degree-6 invariants) = 1** — matching our rank-1 computation
independently. Verdict on step 4: **the invariant space is one-dimensional in
degree 6; O3's second clause is closed.**

**(C) The a-priori constraint — this is what makes the test sharp.**
I₆ is a degree-6 SL₃-invariant in each factor, hence a **GL₃ semi-invariant with
character det²** in each factor (scaling one factor by λI multiplies I₆ by
λ⁶ = det(λI)²). Restricting to slabs (I, A, B):

* the subgroup fixing slab₀ = I acts by A ↦ gAg⁻¹, B ↦ gBg⁻¹ with **trivial**
  character (det(g)²·det(g)⁻² = 1) ⟹ the restriction is a simultaneous-
  conjugation invariant;
* the slab element slab₀ ↦ slab₀ + t·slab₁, renormalised by left-multiplying
  every slab by (I+tA)⁻¹, gives exactly
  **I₆((I+tA)⁻¹A, (I+tA)⁻¹B)·det(I+tA)² = I₆(A,B)**, i.e. session 18's
  functional equation with k = 2, infinitesimally
  **D_A f + 2 tr(A) f = 0** (and A↔B);
* that condition is **bidegree-preserving** (D_A and tr(A)· both raise the
  A-degree by exactly one), so **every bidegree component of I₆|₍I,A,B₎
  satisfies it separately.**

**CORRECTION to session 18 found while setting this up (independent of I₆).**
The doc records the equivariant subspace of the bidegree-(2,2) conjugation
invariants as 2-dimensional, cut to 1 by intersecting with the det²-slot. That
2 is a **coefficient-space artefact**: the 10 spanning trace words are linearly
**dependent** for 3×3 matrices (one relation, the polarised Cayley–Hamilton
identity — rank 9, machine-verified), and the second nullspace vector is exactly
that relation. In **function space** the equivariant bidegree-(2,2) space is
**1-dimensional, spanned by Ψ** (machine-verified: `wk4_s19_slotdim.py`,
generator = −Ψ/12). The det²-slot was never needed; **equivariance alone
characterises Ψ up to scale.** Session 18's theorem is thereby *strengthened*,
not weakened.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## THE PRE-REGISTRATION PROPER

Given (C), the candidate outcomes of step 5 are not equally available:

> **O2 is a-priori IMPOSSIBLE.** The bidegree-(2,2) component of I₆|₍I,A,B₎
> cannot be "a different element of the slot or of the 10-dim space": the
> equivariance it inherits from I₆'s det² character confines it to the
> 1-dimensional space ⟨Ψ⟩. If the computation returns a (2,2) component that is
> **not** a scalar multiple of Ψ, that is **not** outcome O2 — it is a
> **refutation of the character argument or a bug in the machinery**, and must
> be treated as an alarm, not a result.

So the live outcomes are exactly two:

* **O1** — the (2,2) component is a **nonzero** multiple c·Ψ.
* **O3′** — the (2,2) component vanishes identically (c = 0). The framing then
  needs repair: Ψ would be equivariant but invisible to the tensor's generator.

**PREDICTION (logged before computing): O1, with c ≠ 0.**

**Decision rule, fixed in advance.**

1. Compute I₆|₍I,A,B₎ = Σ_{a+b≤6} c_{ab}(A,B) by exact interpolation in the two
   slab scalings (I, xA, yB).
2. **Support test.** Every bidegree whose a-priori equivariant dimension is 0
   must have c_{ab} ≡ 0. Predicted support ⊆ {(6,0),(5,1),(2,2),(1,5),(0,6)}
   ∪ (3,3) ∪ (4,2),(2,4) if those dimensions are nonzero. A nonzero component in
   a dimension-0 bidegree ⟹ ALARM (character argument or machinery wrong), stop.
3. **Identification test.** Fit c₂₂ in the 10-word spanning set; check
   c₂₂ = c·Ψ exactly as polynomials. c ≠ 0 ⟹ **O1**; c = 0 ⟹ **O3′**;
   anything else ⟹ ALARM per the box above.
4. **Banked cross-check (step 6), pre-registered ratios.** With Ψ = 1, 1, 1, 4,
   0, 0 at C, R, T4, X4, P and the compression points, the predicted values of
   c₂₂ at the corresponding pencils are
   **c·(1, 1, 1, 4, 0, 0)** — one scale, six numbers, five ratio constraints.
   Pencils (from `wk3_s16_fit.py`, unchanged): C = (E₂₁, E₁₂), R = (E₀₀, E₁₁),
   T4 = (diag(1,1,0), diag(0,1,1)), X4 = (E₀₀, E₁₂+E₂₁), P = (E₀₀, E₀₀),
   Q = (E₁₀, E₀₁) (Ψ = 1). **Any inconsistency here outranks the
   identification** and is reported first.

**BOUNDARY, stated in advance so no result can be written up as more than it
is.** Even O1 would **not** prove the totals law. It would show that Ψ is the
bidegree-(2,2) component of the tensor's fundamental invariant — an
identification of *which canonical object* Ψ is. It would leave entirely open
**why the evaluation picks out that component**: nothing in this computation
connects the λ′-highest-weight-vector evaluation h₁ to I₆'s (2,2) part. That
remaining step is the actual content of open problem (2).

# PART II — RESULT (computed after the pre-registration was committed, c06ba3f)

## Verdict: **O1**, and in a stronger form than O1 was stated

> **THEOREM (exact symbolic identity, machine-verified on the generic pencil in
> all 18 indeterminates).**
>
>     I₆( I , A , B )  =  −6 · Ψ(A, B)
>
> where I₆ is the complete ε-contraction of T^{⊗6} with pattern
> P₁ = ((0,1,2),(3,4,5)), P₂ = ((0,1,3),(2,4,5)), P₃ = ((0,2,4),(1,3,5)),
> and Ψ = 2u₁ − 4u₂ − D.

Not "the bidegree-(2,2) component of the restriction is a multiple of Ψ" — the
**whole restriction is**, because the restriction has no other component:

> **LEMMA (bidegree rigidity of the restriction; proved, not observed).** Each
> of the two slot-1 ε₃'s absorbs the slab indices of three copies and vanishes
> unless those three indices are **distinct**. Hence in every surviving term
> each of the two 3-blocks uses slab₀, slab₁, slab₂ exactly once, so the slab
> multidegree is forced to (2,2,2): I₆|₍I,A,B₎ is **purely bidegree (2,2)** in
> (A,B). Machine check: the only bidegree occurring in the expanded restriction
> is (2,2).

This also retires the pre-registered support test as vacuous-but-passed: the
predicted support was ⊆ {(6,0),(5,1),(2,2),(1,5),(0,6),(3,3)}; the observed
support is exactly {(2,2)}. No component appeared in a bidegree whose a-priori
equivariant dimension is 0 — no alarm.

## Normalisation: the coefficient is 1, not −6

The contraction's 1152 monomials have coefficients ±{6, 12, 24, 48, 60} with
**content exactly 6**. So the primitive integral form is I₆^prim = I₆/6, and

    **Ψ  =  − I₆^prim( I , A , B )** ,   coefficient 1.

Ψ *is* the Aronhold degree-6 invariant of the net's tensor, in normal form, up
to sign. The −4 that session 18 had to derive from equivariance is simply what
the Aronhold invariant does.

## Three consequences, and one correction to the record

1. **Compression ⟹ 0 becomes classical.** A compression net (generic rank ≤ 2,
   common left kernel) has a degenerate tensor, and its Aronhold invariant
   vanishes. Session 17's 5/5 empirical criterion and session 18's slot-coordinate
   identity are now one classical fact.
2. **χ = Ψ on 120/120 same-coset pairs is now a corollary, not a coincidence.**
   Ψ = −I₆^prim is a tensor semi-invariant with character det² in each factor, so
   it *must* transform multiplicatively along double cosets. The empirical
   agreement was the character it was bound to have.
3. **Ψ is engine-free and basis-free forever.** It is a classical invariant of a
   3-dimensional net of 3×3 matrices, computable from the net alone.
4. **CORRECTION to session 18 (independent of I₆, and it strengthens the
   theorem).** The bidegree-(2,2) simultaneous-conjugation space is
   **9-dimensional, not 10** — the ten trace words satisfy one relation (the
   polarised Cayley–Hamilton identity for 3×3). Session 18's "equivariance
   leaves 2 dimensions, and the det²-slot cuts it to 1" was a coefficient-space
   artefact: the second nullspace vector *is* the relation. In function space
   **equivariance alone leaves exactly 1 dimension, spanned by Ψ.** The
   det²-slot was never needed for the characterisation.

## THE BOUNDARY — what this does NOT establish

**This does not prove the totals law.** It identifies *which* canonical object Ψ
is: the Aronhold invariant of the net's tensor, restricted to slab normal form.
It says nothing about why the λ′-highest-weight-vector **evaluation** h₁ — a
GL₉-Borel object living in Sym²⁰Sym³C⁹, with no tensor in sight — should have
totals proportional to that invariant. TOTAL(N) = Ψ(N)·1,152,144,000 remains an
**empirical law**, tested at Ψ = 1 (C, R), Ψ = 4 (X4) and Ψ = 0 (P and four
compression points). Open problem (2) is *reframed*, not solved.

It is also worth saying what did **not** get easier: the per-σ values. Session
16's refutation stands untouched — at X4 the σ-table reorganises rather than
rescaling, and nothing here bears on it. Ψ governs totals only.

## What the remaining step now looks like (DESIGN, not a result)

The identification does sharpen the target, and the sharpened target is worth
recording precisely, with its gaps named:

* (i) TOTAL is a bidegree-(2,2) polynomial on W = C²⊗gl₃ — session 15's counting
  lemma (t = (2,2)). **Proved, prior session.**
* (ii) TOTAL is constant on the double cosets Q·u·H up to χ, and the double coset
  of u_N is the H-orbit of the annihilator net *as a subspace* of M₃ — session
  17's theorem. **Proved, prior session; not re-derived here.**
* (iii) Because only the **subspace** matters, the net's basis change (the third
  tensor slot) acts trivially, and the two M₃-side actions are the other two
  slots. So TOTAL, written in the standard parametrisation (I, A, B), would be a
  simultaneous-conjugation invariant satisfying a slab equivariance with
  character χ. **NOT verified this session** — in particular the transpose coset
  and the exact slot dictionary need checking.
* (iv) IF that character is det², then Part I's uniqueness (equivariance alone
  pins the bidegree-(2,2) space to ⟨Ψ⟩) forces TOTAL = c·Ψ, and evaluating at C
  gives c = 1,152,144,000 — the totals law, proved. **The single unproved link
  is χ ↔ det².** Session 17 verified χ = Ψ on 120/120 same-coset pairs
  numerically; that is evidence for exactly this identification, not a proof of
  it.

So the honest state is: the totals law now has a *candidate proof* with one
named gap, where before it had none. That is progress on open problem (2), and
it is not a solution to it. Nothing in this document should be written up as
more.
