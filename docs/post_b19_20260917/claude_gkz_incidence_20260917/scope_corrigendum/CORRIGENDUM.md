## C1. Theorem A, consequence clause (parent section 3.B, line 110)

**Quoted.** "Consequently every minor of every Macaulay matrix `M_k` that vanishes on `closure(GL_16 . det_4)` vanishes on `closure(GL_16 . z per_3)`, in every degree and every `GL_16`-type, i.e. in every cell `(d, lambda)` of every length."

**Corrected.** For every internal grading `k >= 0`, `r_pad(k) <= r_det(k)` (this is the proved inequality, unchanged), and consequently

    J_k  ⊆  I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)      for every k,   Y_det = closure(GL_16 . det_4),  Y_pad = closure(GL_16 . z per_3).

The containment covers, without further argument: (i) every minor of `M_k` of size `> r_det(k)`, since the ideal of `s`-minors is contained in the ideal of `s'`-minors for `s >= s'` (Laplace expansion of a larger minor along a row expresses it as a polynomial combination of smaller minors); (ii) every polynomial combination and multiple of these generators, i.e. the whole ideal `J_k`; (iii) every `GL_16`-isotypic component of `J_k` in every cell `(d, lambda)` of every length, because `J_k` is `GL_16`-stable and `I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)` is a `GL_16`-stable ideal; (iv) the sum `sum_k J_k`, and the ideal it generates. It does **not** establish that an individual minor of size `<= r_det(k)` which happens to vanish on `Y_det`, or a linear combination of such minors vanishing on `Y_det`, also vanishes on `Y_pad`; no such statement is proved anywhere in the parent, and the parent's sentence must be read as the rank-threshold statement above.

**Reason.** Lemma 3.1 of the parent proves only that minors of size `r_det(k) + 1` vanish on `Y_det` (rank semicontinuity) and Lemma 3.2 that they vanish on `Y_pad` when `r_pad(k) <= r_det(k)`. A minor of size `<= r_det(k)` can vanish identically on `Y_det` for reasons unrelated to rank (a zero pattern, a coincidence of the coefficient support), and nothing in the rank comparison controls its value on `Y_pad`. The stronger sentence was an overstatement of the same proof.

**Changes.** No theorem changes: the inequality `r_pad(k) <= r_det(k)` for all `k`, its Gulliksen-Negard dependency and the P1 certificates (`u(0..9)`, the `k <= 10` table, the Newton differences) stand unchanged. No certificate changes. The practical decision (close the rank-threshold route) stands; its statement is narrowed to `J_k`.

## C5. "The whole complex is subject to the same wrong-way comparison" (parent line 274)

**Quoted.** "the observation that the *whole* complex, not just its first differential, is subject to the same wrong-way comparison (`dim (S/J_F)_k` and its syzygy analogues are larger on padding)."

**Corrected.** Withdrawn. What is established is: (a) the first differential, for every `k` (Theorems A, B, rank-threshold sense); (b) the second differential `D_2` at the two internal gradings tested exactly, `k = 6` (rank 120 at `det_4`, no rank drop, so no determinant equation; 105 at `z per_3`) and `k = 7` (rank 1904 of 1920 at `det_4`; 1650 at `z per_3`), exact over `Q` (pilot P3), so that at `k = 7` the ideal of size-1905 minors of `D_7` lies in `I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)` by the `D_k`-version of Lemma 3.1-3.2. Nothing is established for `D_2` at `k >= 8` or for `D_j`, `j >= 3`, at any `k`.

**Why the first-differential comparison does not determine the higher ranks.** In internal grading `k` the Koszul complex has terms `K_j = Lambda^j C^N (x) S_{k-3j}` (dimensions independent of `F`), differentials `d_j`, and homology `H_j`. Elementary linear algebra gives, for each `j >= 1`,

    rank d_j = dim K_j - dim ker d_j = dim K_j - rank d_{j+1} - dim H_j,

hence `rank d_1 = dim K_1 - rank d_2 - dim H_1`. The first-differential comparison (Theorems A, B) fixes `rank d_1` on each family, i.e. it fixes the sum `rank d_2 + dim H_1`, not its two summands. Two forms with `rank d_1(pad) <= rank d_1(det)` can have `rank d_2(pad) > rank d_2(det)` provided `dim H_1(pad) < dim H_1(det)` by a larger amount, and similarly at every stage: `rank d_j` depends on `dim H_j, ..., dim H_{N}` (alternating sums), which are the graded Betti-type data of `J_F` and are not functions of the Hilbert function `dim (S/J_F)_k = dim K_0 - rank d_1`. So the ordering of higher-differential ranks is a separate question at each `(j, k)`, decided in this packet only where P3 computed it.

**Changes.** No certificate changes (P3's numbers stand). The claim about the whole complex is withdrawn; the assessed range is stated in C7.

## C8. "The GKZ toolbox contains exactly three things" and "it cannot" (parent lines 11, 274)

**Quoted.** "the GKZ toolbox contains exactly three things that act on a single quartic's coefficients, and none of them produces a determinant equation that is both new to the programme and nonzero on padding" (line 11); "It adds no new determinant equation nonzero on padding, and it cannot: the three GKZ mechanisms are the discriminant (blind), Fitting ideals of the discriminant complex (blind, Theorems A-B and P3), and toric/face structure (not `GL`-invariant). The one GKZ object that separates in the right direction, the dual variety of `X_F`, is LMR and was already in the census." (line 274).

**Corrected.** Neither the survey nor this session classifies every GKZ-inspired construction applicable to the problem. The session assessed five specific constructions, with these outcomes:

- A. The ordinary discriminant (the `A`-discriminant for the full quartic support, the dual of the Veronese) vanishes on the whole product family `{ ell C }` and on `Y_pad`: blind (elementary, `dF = C dl + l dC`).
- B. The dual-*dimension* / Hessian-rank construction examined (conditions `dim X_F^vee <= k`, read through `rank Hess F` on `X_F`) reproduces the known LMR / Mignon-Ressayre mechanism recorded in `equation_census.md`; it was not developed further here.
- C. The Macaulay rank-threshold construction (`J_k`, first Koszul differential) is closed for separation by Theorems A and B in the sense of C1-C2.
- D. The second differential at the two internal gradings tested (`k = 6, 7`, sixteen variables) is blind in the rank-threshold sense (P3).
- E. The toric coefficient-scaling / face tests considered (principal `A`-determinant of the 24-permutation support, face initial forms of `det_4`) did not supply a justified new `GL_16`-stable determinant equation in this session; the specific objection recorded is that torus-singularity and face factors are coordinate-dependent and that `z per_3` is not in the sparse family.

Not inferred: that richer dual geometry (higher associated hypersurfaces, tangency loci, Chow forms of singular schemes with their multiplicity structure), other incidence schemes, other complexes (e.g. complexes of other sheaves on `X_F`, or the full complex beyond the tested differentials), or every `GL`-equivariant construction obtainable from toric data has been ruled out. Three different statuses apply and are kept apart in `CLAIM_SCOPE_TABLE.md`: *absent* (not constructed in this session), *excluded by a theorem* (C, and A, D in their stated ranges), and *not funded now* (a decision, not a theorem).

**Reason.** "Exactly three", "it cannot" and "the one GKZ object" are exhaustiveness claims with no proof; the survey is a guide to one book, and the session examined one construction in depth.

**Changes.** No theorem or certificate changes. The decision (do not fund a GKZ route without a concrete mechanism) stands but is no longer justified by exhaustiveness.
