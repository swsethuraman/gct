# Revised verdict for `work/claude_gkz_incidence_20260917` (replaces the parent's section 0 and section 8)

17 September 2026. This text stands on its own; the parent report supplies the proofs, scripts and certificates it refers to, and `CORRIGENDUM.md` in this directory records each change of wording.

## Question

Can a GKZ-style construction (discriminant, resultant, Chow form, or a complex whose rank drop yields equations) produce a globally certified equation of the `det_4` orbit closure that carries more than "the hypersurface is singular" and more than product structure, and that could eventually support a determinant-versus-padded-permanent multiplicity obstruction?

## Outcome

**C: a rigorous negative for the one construction selected and assessed, with two exact side results; no gap, no cell, no exhaustive claim about GKZ.**

## What is proved (unchanged from the parent)

Notation: `M_k(F)` is the Macaulay matrix of the partials of a quartic `F` in internal grading `k` (columns indexed by degree-`k` monomials; entries linear in `F`); `r_det(k)` is its maximal rank on the determinant closure `Y_det`; `J_k` is the ideal generated by the `(r_det(k)+1)`-minors, polynomials of degree `r_det(k)+1` in the quartic coefficients.

- **Theorem A (sixteen variables).** For every `k`, `rank M_k(z per_3) <= rank M_k(det_4)`. Dependencies: the Gulliksen-Negard resolution of the submaximal minors of the generic `4 x 4` matrix (adopted), and exact integer certificates produced in pilot P1 (the Hilbert function of the `2 x 2` permanental ideal through degree 9 and a Newton forward-difference certificate for all `k >= 11`).
- **Theorem B (five variables).** For every `k`, the maximal rank of `M_k` on the product cone `P5 = { ell C }` is at most its maximal rank on `D45`, the closure of quinary `det_4` pencils. Dependencies: Kleiman transversality (adopted, as in the onset note) for `k >= 7`; exact rank certificates at an explicit integer pencil (pilot P2) for `k <= 6`.
- **Consequence (rank-threshold sense).** `J_k ⊆ I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)` for every `k`, in both settings. This covers all larger minors, all polynomial combinations, every `GL`-module component in every cell, and the sum over `k`. It does not say anything about individual minors of size `<= r_det(k)` that happen to vanish on the determinant, nor about their linear combinations.
- **Second differential, two cases (exact over `Q`, pilot P3, sixteen variables).** At internal grading 6 the second Koszul differential has full rank 120 at `det_4` (no determinant equation there); at internal grading 7 it has rank 1904 of 1920 at `det_4` and 1650 at `z per_3`, so the ideal of its size-1905 minors lies in `I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)`.

## What this means for multiplicities

In every cell, the equations supplied by `J_k` (and by the tested second-differential ideal) lie in the common ideal `I(Y_det) ∩ I(Y_pad)`. They supply no separating equation and no advantage in the difference `D = i_det - i_pad`. They do not show that `D = 0` in any cell; `D` is governed by the parts of the two ideals outside the common ideal, which this packet does not address. No positive gap is claimed and no cell is nominated.

## How this relates to GKZ

The Cayley method of GKZ computes the discriminant as the determinant of the Koszul complex of the partials pushed down to coefficient space; the Macaulay matrices are the graded pieces of the first differential of that complex. So the construction closed here is the GKZ complex read through the Fitting ideals of its first differential, plus two tested cases of its second differential. The identification is the session's contribution; the construction itself was already the programme's Jacobian-cap / Macaulay-minor family (Proposition D of the record, proved there for at most six variables given measured coranks). Theorem B removes the measured dependency at five variables; Theorem A extends the containment to the full sixteen-variable ambient and hence to cells of every length.

## What was examined and found not new, and what was not examined

- The ordinary discriminant vanishes on all products `ell C`: blind, elementary.
- The dual-dimension / Hessian-rank construction examined (`dim X_F^vee <= k` through minors of `Hess F`) reproduces the LMR / Mignon-Ressayre mechanism already in the programme's equation census. Richer dual geometry of `X_F` (class, singularities of the dual, higher tangency loci, Chow forms of `Sing X_F`) was not assessed.
- The toric coefficient-scaling and face tests considered (principal `A`-determinant of the permutation support; face initial forms of `det_4`) did not supply a justified `GL_16`-stable determinant equation in this session. Independently, the Astra session `work/astra_gkz_degenerations_20260917` proves that, for block-scalar weights in the fixed `(a, r, c, S, v)` decomposition, all universal forbidden-weight and exact-exponent-support source tests factor through the existing arc `C`; its nine classes are initial-form classes of that restricted family. That is a rigorous negative for that weight family, not for toric methods in general.
- Not assessed: the second differential at internal grading `>= 8`; all differentials `d_j`, `j >= 3`; the second differential in five variables; the `GL`-decomposition of any of these Fitting ideals; other incidence schemes and complexes; any GKZ-inspired construction not named above. Neither the survey read nor this session classifies all GKZ-inspired constructions applicable to the problem.

## Reconciliation of "no five-row determinant equations are known"

Five-row `det_4` equations are on the record: the size-300 minors of the internal-grading-7 Macaulay matrix of quinary quartics, polynomial degree 300 (onset note, Theorem 1 at `n = 4`, proved there modulo Kleiman, Dimca and Gulliksen-Negard). The construction producing them is contained in `I(D45) ∩ I(P5)` by Theorem B, so none of them separates padding. The accurate statement is "no five-row determinant equation known to be nonzero on padding", not "none known". The batch-13 onset bracket for `delta_0` refers to the ideal of `det_3` quinary cubics, not to `D45`; no numerical onset bound for `D45` is used or asserted here.

## Decision

Close, for separation purposes, the all-`k` Macaulay rank-threshold route (`J_k`, five and sixteen variables) and the two tested second-differential cases (sixteen variables, internal gradings 6 and 7). Do not expand their large minors. Everything else named above is retained as unassessed, with no automatic authorization and no recommendation to pursue it. A future proposal on this side must name a concrete mechanism and explain why it escapes the proved exclusions: the rank-threshold containments here and, for toric weight tests in the five-block family, the Astra factorization theorem.
