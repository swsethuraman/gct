## Why the balanced cells and not more degree

Session 43 closed the reachable region at `δ = 7, 8` and opened `δ = 9`: 123 of
123 eligible cells at `n_χ ≤ 20,000`, plus 40 at `δ = 9`, `mult_det = a` at every
one — but every one of them is *peaked* (balance 10–24).  Session 45 measured
balances 11 down to 4 and found the same.  The determinant's six-row ideal has
never switched on, and the region where it plausibly first does is the balanced
one: the stabiliser reduction divides by `|Stab_W(λ)|`, which is smallest exactly
where the parts of `λ` are close together, so those cells have the largest `n_χ`
and were unreachable until now.  **The bracket moved in balance and not in
degree; balance is where the tool opened.**

## Phase 2 — `(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7`, the named target

`N_S = 10,060,304`, `|Stab| = 120`, balance 4, `n_χ ≈ 83,836`, `λ_1 = 8 ≥ δ` so
**obstruction-eligible** — the most balanced eligible `δ = 7` cell there is.
Session 45 enumerated its monomials in 11 s and was stopped by the isotypic
reduction: `2|Stab| = 240` passes over a ten-million-row array.

Two independent ways through, and you should cost both before choosing:

1. **Generators instead of group elements.**  `Stab_W(λ)` is a Young subgroup
   `∏_B S_{k_B}`, generated by adjacent transpositions — 4 of them at `|Stab| =
   120`, not 120.  The orbit partition can be built by iterating *generators* to a
   fixed point (union–find over the monomial index array), which is
   `O(N_S · #generators · α)` rather than `O(N_S · |Stab|)`; the twisted
   coefficient `acc[j] = Σ_g χ(g)[g·rep_j = j]` is then a per-orbit sum, and the
   total work over all orbits is `O(N_S)` because orbit sizes sum to `N_S`.  Walk
   each orbit with generators carrying the sign, and assert consistency (a sign
   conflict on a cycle means the twisted sum vanishes and the orbit drops — which
   is the existing `acc = 0` test, reached more cheaply).  **Validate this against
   `wk9_s45_build.py` on at least six cells with `|Stab|` from 4 to 48 — identical
   orbits, identical signs up to the per-orbit global sign, identical `n_χ`, rows,
   `nnz` and row space — before using it anywhere new.**
2. **Blocking over `N_S`.**  The existing passes are embarrassingly parallel over
   row blocks; if more than two cores are available, use them.

Then measure the cell: `nullity_p([E; ev_det])` at both house primes, `a` by
plethysm, level `(12,2)` from the start (`n_rows/n_χ` will be well above 10).
