## The question

Can `mult_pad` be measured on the **same** source multiplicity space the
determinant side uses, so that the two kernels can be compared and not merely
their dimensions?

## Why this session exists

`D = mult_pad − mult_det = i_det − i_pad`.  The determinant side has an
instrument.  The padded side does not — **but the gap is smaller than the batch
plan says**, and finding that out is the first thing you should confirm.

`analysis/wk9_s60_cell.py` already computes both sides of a cell as

    mult_det = a − nullity_Q [E; ev_det]
    mult_red = a − nullity_Q [E; ev_red]

with `E` the stacked simple raising operators on the `χ_λ`-isotypic reduction
(`analysis/wk9_s45_build.py`, unchanged), `ev_det` the evaluation rows at random
`det_4` pencils, and `ev_red` the same at random reducible points `ℓ(s)·c(s)`.
**The common-source architecture that the plan asks for already exists.**  What
is missing is one more evaluation family:

    mult_pad = a − nullity_Q [E; ev_pad],   ev_pad at points ℓ(s)·per_3(A s)

`analysis/pad.py` already builds `x_0 · per_3` machinery (session 26's
reduction, `per_3^pad` in `Sym^4 C^16` using 10 of 16 coordinates, source
`(C^10)^r`), and `tools/verify/testset/padded_permanent.json` and
`padded_permanent_r6.json` already supply padded points in the declared format.

So this session is a bounded extension of a working engine, not a greenfield
build.  **Confirm that reading in your first hour.**  If it is wrong — if `E`
does not in fact carry over, or the padded points are not in the right ambient —
say so immediately and clearly, because the batch's critical path is drawn on
the assumption that it is right.

## The containment you must respect

The reducible variety `R_r` — closures of `ℓ·c` with `c` a generic cubic —
**contains** the padded-permanent orbit closure, since `per_3` is one particular
cubic.  Hence

    I(R_r) ⊆ I(pad)   ⟹   i_red ≤ i_pad   ⟹   mult_red ≥ mult_pad

That inequality is your primary calibration: at every cell where `i_red` is
banked, your `i_pad` must be at least `i_red`.  A violation is a defect in the
implementation, not a discovery.  It also explains why the record measures
`i_red`: it is a cheaper necessary condition, since `D ≤ i_det − i_red`.

## Tasks

1. Confirm the architecture reading above.  Report it either way.
2. Implement `ev_pad` — evaluation rows at random points `ℓ(s)·per_3(A(s))`
   with `ℓ` a random linear form and `A` a random `3×3` matrix of linear forms,
   in `r` variables — in the same coordinates and the same `E` as `ev_det` and
   `ev_red`.
3. **Calibrate at low degree** against every cell where `mult_red` is banked,
   checking `mult_pad ≤ mult_red` at each, with equality where the reducible
   locus is already generated by permanents.
4. **Calibrate on the `r = 5` cells where `P_5 = R_5`** and session 60 supplies
   exact multiplicities.  Agreement there is the strongest available check,
   because the two families coincide and the two engines must return the same
   number.
5. Verify rank agreement across at least two independent constructions or seeds
   at every calibration cell.  Random evaluation gives a lower bound on the true
   rank; make the seed count explicit and report the margin.
6. **Run the degeneracy-direction pre-check** (`brief_wording.md` §5,
   `tools/verify/testset/degeneracy_check.py`) on the finished evaluator: a
   `det_4` pencil, a reducible `ℓ·c` with `c` generic, and the **full
   ten-variable** `ℓ·per_3` — not a restriction to fewer variables, which can
   manufacture accidental vanishing.
7. **Preserve explicit kernel coordinates in `M_λ`.**  Session 65 compares
   `U_D = ker T_det` against `U_P = ker T_pad` inside the same space and needs
   bases, not dimensions.
8. Report the cost curve in `r` and `δ`, and state plainly whether `r = 9`,
   `δ = 23, 24` is reachable by this route.  That is what session 65 is waiting
   for.

## Success

Exact agreement with every banked reducible and padded multiplicity, a passed
degeneracy pre-check, kernel coordinates preserved, and a measured cost curve
out to the LMR scale or an honest statement of where it stops.

## Stopping rules

- **Any unexplained mismatch with a banked multiplicity stops LMR use
  immediately.**  A padded engine that disagrees anywhere is not usable at the
  one cell that matters, and reporting the mismatch is more valuable than
  working around it.
- If `mult_pad > mult_red` anywhere, that is the containment inequality
  violated: stop and find the defect.
- Do not attempt the LMR cell in this session.  Calibration is the deliverable.
