## Decisions, controls and falsifiers

1. Inventory success: all old unparsed paths receive a nonempty classification,
   provenance and cell-key disposition; fresh frozen JSON/JSONL coverage is
   reported separately. Never equate zero parsing backlog with zero proof backlog.
2. Certificate audit success: every manifest entry appears exactly once with
   current presence, digest result, historical reason and replay status. Duplicates,
   missing prerequisite sources or malformed fields are reported, never skipped.
3. Recovery: attempt up to four cheapest eligible missing certificates/cells or
   exact substitute witnesses, chosen by recorded size and available generators.
   A record-only file may be recovered without being upgraded. If heavy producer
   dependencies are absent, prefer a bounded exact small witness derived from
   banked data; otherwise ship an explicit priced recovery queue.
4. Index updates: append source-backed identities and precise exclusions to both
   human and machine indexes. Keep structural identities, funding advice and
   containment conclusions distinct from empty-ideal assertions. Use explicit
   application context and controls at every new predicate boundary.
5. Deliberate bad inputs: empty inventory, removed source, wrong cell degree,
   wrong digest, false supersession, changed matrix entry and missing certificate
   must reject. For recovered arithmetic also use a forced-zero/degenerate case.
   A failed control halts promotion, while inventory work can continue.
