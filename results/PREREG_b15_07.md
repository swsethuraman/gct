## Mathematical question and convention

Over Q, let V=Q^3 and A=Sym(Sym^4 V), the ordinary coefficient polynomial
ring in 15 variables c_alpha, |alpha|=4. Forms lie in Sym^4(V*).
E_ij c_alpha=(alpha_i+1)c_(alpha+e_i-e_j) for i!=j and alpha_j>0;
E_ii c_alpha=alpha_i c_alpha. Extend by derivations. Bracket letters use
m_alpha=alpha! c_alpha, with no additional equal-letter factorial.

Construct a nonzero non-Cartan map P_nu composed with multiplication on
actual polynomial modules, initially (A_2)_(6,2,0) tensor A_1 ->
(A_3)_(6,4,2). Here parentheses denote the full isotypic submodule, not
only the highest-weight line. First-row-only products of two HW vectors
cannot exercise this channel: lowered source/multiplier vectors are needed.

The proposed equivalent representation-theoretic procedure is the central
quadratic Casimir C=sum_ij E_ij E_ji. On S_lambda Q^r it has eigenvalue
sum_i lambda_i(lambda_i+r+1-2i). For a degree whose complete partition
envelope separates nu, use the Lagrange polynomial which is one at its
eigenvalue and zero at every other possible eigenvalue. Reject collisions
with a distinct partition: C alone then specifies only their combined
eigenspace. Do not split an isotypic multiplicity space arbitrarily.

Prove centrality, normalization, equivariance, descent through polynomial
relations, and preservation of every homogeneous GL-stable ideal. Also
