## 1. The lemma being implemented (stated before validation)

Let `lam` have blocks of equal parts with sizes `k_1..k_t` and values
`m_1..m_t`, so `Stab_W(lam) = S_{k_1} x ... x S_{k_t}` acts on the `lam`-weight
space of `C[W]_delta` by permuting variable indices within blocks.  Every
highest-weight vector `v` of weight `lam` satisfies

    P_w v = chi_lam(w) v,     chi_lam(w) = prod_B sgn(w_B)^{m_B}.

*Proof.*  `P_w v` has weight `w(lam) = lam` and lies in the irreducible
`V_lam` generated by `v`, whose `lam`-weight space is one-dimensional, so
`P_w v = c(w) v` with `c` a character of `Stab_W(lam)` intrinsic to `V_lam`
(any two copies are `G`-isomorphic and `P_w` is in `G`); on the leading-minor
model `prod_k Delta_k^{lam_k − lam_{k+1}}` a transposition `(i, i+1)` inside a
block of value `m` negates every `Delta_k` with `k >= i+1`, total
`(−1)^{lam_{i+1}} = (−1)^m`.  At rectangular `lam` this is s33's `sign^delta`
lemma.  ∎

Consequence: `HWV_lam ⊆ V_chi` (the `chi_lam`-isotypic component), of dimension
`n_chi ~ N_S / |Stab|`.  Pipeline: twisted orbit sums as basis of `V_chi`
(orbits whose twisted sum vanishes drop out), **every** simple raising
operator restricted to `V_chi`, rows deduplicated by the common stabiliser
`H = Stab(lam) ∩ Stab(lam + e_i − e_{i+1})` (rows in one `H`-orbit are
proportional by `chi`; `chi`-obstructed `H`-fixed rows must vanish — asserted
exactly, a free check), kernel by the certified compressed route of
`docs/e4_hunt.md` §4 (`rank(Agg) <= rank_p(M) <= rank_Q(M) = n_chi − a`, the
assert `dim ker(Agg) = a` forcing equality), exact single-`rref` route for
small cells and for validation.  **Porting note, committed in advance:** s33's
one-operator shortcut (`ker E_12` alone) needs an even-conjugator set that is
2-transitive; a general Young subgroup is not, so all `r − 1` simple operators
are applied.  Only the isotypic restriction generalises.

**Point construction.**  det points: `det_4(sum_{i<=r} s_i A_i)`, random
`4x4` integer `A_i` (entries in `[−40, 40]`).  pad points at every `r`: the
**true padded-permanent restriction** `x_0 · per_3(x_1..x_9)` with each `x_t`
a random linear form in `s_1..s_r` (`per_padded(3,4)` through `restrict()`,
as the house pipeline has always done) — never `l · (random cubic)`, which
coincides with it at `r = 5` (`per_3`-pencils are dense in quinary cubics)
but **not** at `r = 6` (`dim D_6^{per_3} = 50 < 56`), where it would
over-estimate `mult_pad` and could manufacture a false `D > 0`.
