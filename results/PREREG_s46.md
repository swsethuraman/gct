## 0. What this session is for

and reached balance 4 for the first time.  It stopped at one named cell:
`(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7`, `N_S = 10,060,304`, `|Stab| = 120`, balance 4,

## 2. The one engineering change, and what would falsify it

**The generator build (`analysis/wk9_s46_gen.py`).**  `Stab_W(λ) = ∏_B S_{k_B}`
is generated by the adjacent transpositions inside its blocks — 4 of them at
`|Stab| = 120`, not 120 — and each such generator has
`χ_λ(transposition in a block of value m) = (−1)^m`.  Instead of `2|Stab|`
passes over the `(N_S × δ)` monomial array, the build computes the image index
array once per **generator** (so `#gen` passes, not `2|Stab|`), then:

- **orbits** by min-label propagation over the generator edges (each generator is
  an involution, so the edges are a matching), iterated to a fixed point;
- **signs** by carrying `χ` along the same edges: `s[g·m] = χ(g)·s[m]`, anchored
  at `s[orbit representative] = +1`;
- **the drop test** as a final consistency pass: an orbit is dropped exactly when
  some generator edge inside it has `χ(g)·s[m] ≠ s[g·m]`.

The last item is the same test as s45's `acc[j] = 0`, reached more cheaply:
a sign conflict on a closed walk from the representative is exactly an element
`h ∈ Stab_rep` with `χ(h) = −1`, which is exactly `Σ_{h∈Stab_rep} χ(h) = 0`.
Total work `O(N_S · #gen · rounds)` with `rounds` bounded by the generator-graph
diameter of a single orbit (`≤ |Stab|`, in practice ~10), against
`O(N_S · |Stab|)`.

**Predicted equality with s45's build (P2a).**  The generator build returns
*identical* `n_χ`, identical orbit partition, identical `col_of`, identical
`sgn` (not merely up to a per-orbit global sign — both anchor `+1` at the
minimum-index representative), identical `obstructed`, identical `E` row count,
`nnz` and row space, at **every** validation cell.

**Falsifier.**  Any disagreement in `n_χ`, the partition, `obstructed`, the row
space, or a `sgn` mismatch not attributable to a documented convention, at any
of the validation cells, stops the new build being used anywhere new.  The
session then falls back to s45's build and reports the balanced band it can
reach with it.

**Validation set (fixed here, before it is run).**  At least six cells with
`|Stab|` from 4 to 48, spanning one and two nontrivial blocks and both parities
of block value, plus the trivial-stabiliser case:

    (9,9,6,2,1,1)_7   |Stab| = 4     (12,12,3,3,1,1)_8  |Stab| = 8
    (9,9,4,4,1,1)_7   |Stab| = 8     (8,8,6,2,2,2)_7    |Stab| = 12
    (9,9,9,3,1,1)_8   |Stab| = 12    (6,6,6,6,2,2)_7    |Stab| = 48
    (13,10,6,1,1,1)_8 |Stab| = 6     (12,9,3,2,1,1)_7   |Stab| = 2

and, for the `H`-orbit row dedup (the second place `_canon_acc` is called), the
same comparison on every target basis of every operator at each of those cells.

## 5. P3 — the determinant side, and the protocol if it drops

**P3 (prediction): `mult_det = a` at every cell measured this session.
Confidence 90 % that no cell shows `mult_det < a`; equivalently ~10 % that the
six-row determinant ideal switches on somewhere in the `δ = 7` balanced band.**

*Reasoning, and the regime it applies to.*  The record entering this session is
188 distinct cells / 585 ambient units across `δ = 6–9` with `mult_det = a` at
every one, and s45 moved balance from 8 to 4 without changing that.  Against
that, three things keep the probability off the floor: (i) every one of those
188 cells is *peaked* or nearly so, and the s30/s36 dimension heuristic places a
first det-side bite in exactly the near-rectangular corner this session enters;
(ii) `(8,4,4,4,4,4)` is the most balanced eligible weight in the whole `δ = 7`
region and has never been probed by anything; (iii) the onset is bracketed only
from below in reach.  Against a drop, and why the number is 90 % and not 60 %:
session 44 proved from the Gulliksen–Negard ceiling that the six-row onset
`I(D_6^{det_4}) ≤ 1197` a priori and `≤ 666` at explicit pencils, and proved *no
drop at `d ≤ 6`* — a bracket `[9, 1197]` whose lower end the sweeps keep pushing
and whose width makes a first switch-on at `δ = 7` a small slice of the
available range.  The prediction is therefore *regime-limited*: it is a
prediction about `δ = 7` (and the two `δ = 7` onset-only cells), not about the
onset degree.

**Verification protocol if `nullity_p([E; ev_det]) > 0` at any cell — copied
verbatim from the brief:**

> **A nullity from a compressed level is never the answer**: session 45's
> `(8,8,6,2,2,2)_7` came out exactly 57 short of full column rank at both primes
> with independent randomness and was pure sampling loss.  Exhibit the kernel
> vectors, verify `E v = 0` against the **uncompressed** `[E; ev]`, re-run at a
> fresh preconditioner, a fresh seed and a second prime, then lift to exact
> integer vectors (`analysis/wk9_s42_lift.py`).  Only then measure the pad side
> at that cell (`mult_pad` at true padded-permanent points, `mult_red` by (★),
> and `h_pad` which is free).  If `D = mult_pad − mult_det > 0`, halt the sweep,
> write `docs/OBSTRUCTION_CANDIDATE.md`, and end the session there.

Nothing is reported as a determinant-side drop that has not been through every
step of that protocol, in that order.

## 6. Phase 3 — the sweep order, fixed by rule here

`results/s46_reach.md` is published **before any Phase-3 cell is measured**: the
`δ = 7` six-row census rebuilt with the measured cost laws
(`nnz ≈ 3.5 N_S`; a cell costs `≈ 10.6·10⁻⁹ · n_χ · nnz_c` seconds), **re-fitted
if Phase 2's build changes them**, with `N_S`, `|Stab|`, `n_χ`, balance, `a` and
a predicted cost for every eligible cell.  The reach table is an **expectation**,
not a measurement, and is labelled so: it assumes fitted `nnz_c/n_χ` and
ns-per-op at cells nobody has built.

**The order, committed here:** after Phase 2, sweep the `δ = 7` cells
**ascending in predicted cost, most balanced first at each size**, skipping cells
already banked by s36/s41/s43/s45.  The two remaining `λ_1 < δ` onset-only
`δ = 7` cells get one cell each if they fit the budget — they cannot carry an
obstruction (`λ_1 < δ ⇒ mult_pad = 0`, Corollary B of `docs/reducible_ideal.md`)
but can carry the determinant ideal, and they are the most balanced weights in
the region.  Each cell is banked with a commit before the next is started.

`h_pad` (s42's normalisation bound) is computed for every cell measured — it
costs milliseconds and the s45 review flagged it as free coverage left on the
table.

## 7. Stopping rules

1. **Validation failure** (any part of §3) ⇒ stop, report, measure nothing new.
2. **`mult_det < a` at any cell** ⇒ the §5 protocol in full; if it survives to
   `D = mult_pad − mult_det > 0`, halt the sweep, write
   `docs/OBSTRUCTION_CANDIDATE.md`, end the session.
3. **Memory** ⇒ a cell that exceeds the container is not retried at a coarser
   setting and is not reported as measured; it is recorded as out of reach and
   the reach table bounds the honesty of the coverage claim.
4. **Primes disagree** on a nullity ⇒ that cell is not banked; re-run at fresh
   randomness, and if they still disagree, report it as an open discrepancy.
5. Every long run is bounded at launch with `timeout` and `ulimit -v`, its
   process id written to `results/logs/<run>.pid`, and ended only by that
   recorded id.

## 8. What this session will *not* claim

- Nothing about degrees above `δ = 8`; nothing about the onset degree beyond the
  bracket the sweeps push from below and s44's Theorem A caps from above.
- No coverage fraction quoted flat: the reach table's cost columns are an
  expectation and are quoted as one.
- A `mult_det = a` row says the determinant ideal is empty *in that cell*, so
  `mult_pad ≤ a = mult_det` forces `D ≤ 0` there.  It says nothing about
  neighbouring cells.
