## Theorem

Let pi2:W5 -> A^15 retain exactly the ordinary coefficients a,p_i,q_ij defined in CONSTRUCTION.md. Then pi2 composed with phi has a rational section over the open set a!=0. Consequently

    Q[a,p_i,q_ij] -> Q[X],  H -> H composed with pi2 composed with phi

is injective. In particular K_d intersect U_d=0 for every d>=0, including the frozen range 1<=d<=8. The same statements hold after extension to C. No nonzero determinant coefficient equation can be a polynomial only in this single framed two-jet. The statement is about this subring, not the entire coefficient ring.

## 1. Four hyperbolic pairs, explicitly

Let (u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4)=(E12,E13,E14,E23), and v_i=u_i^T. Matrix multiplication Eab Ecd=delta_bc Ead gives

    tr(u_i u_j)=tr(v_i v_j)=0,   tr(u_i v_j)=delta_ij.

Both u_i and v_i have trace zero. For any symmetric 4x4 matrix G over any Q-algebra define

    D_i = u_i + (1/2) sum_j G_ij v_j.

Direct bilinear expansion, using symmetry of G, gives tr(D_i)=0 and tr(D_i D_j)=G_ij. This realizes an arbitrary symmetric Gram matrix, including every degenerate one. No square root, positive-definiteness argument, diagonalization or extension of the rational ground field is needed.

## 2. All prescribed second jets occur on a!=0

Use the ring R=Q[a,a^-1,p_1,...,p_4,q_ij (i<=j)]. Put

    r_i=p_i/a,
    G_ii=3 r_i^2/4 - 2 q_ii/a,
    G_ij=3 r_i r_j/4 - q_ij/a       (i<j),
    G_ji=G_ij,
    C_i=(r_i/4) I4 + D_i.

Thus tr(C_i)=r_i and tr(C_i C_j)=r_i r_j/4+G_ij. The identity

    det(x1 I4+Y) = x1^4 + x1^3 tr(Y)
                  + (x1^2/2)((tr Y)^2-tr(Y^2)) + terms of y-degree >=3

for Y=sum_i y_i C_i follows directly from the determinant expansion: the degree-two term is the sum over pairs k<l of (Y_kk Y_ll-Y_kl Y_lk). This sum is exactly the displayed half-trace expression. Thus no Newton-identity literature is a premise.

The coefficient of x1^2 y_i^2 is (r_i^2-tr(C_i^2))/2=q_ii/a. For i<j the ordinary coefficient of x1^2 y_i y_j is r_i r_j-tr(C_i C_j)=q_ij/a. The factor of two differs between the diagonal and mixed coefficients; this is essential.

Set P_a=diag(a,1,1,1), B1=P_a and B_(i+1)=P_a C_i. Determinant multiplicativity gives

    det(sum_k x_k B_k)=a det(x1 I4+sum_i y_i C_i).

Its selected coefficients are exactly (a,p_i,q_ij). This supplies the promised section sigma:Spec R -> X, with

    pi2 composed with phi composed with sigma = id_(a!=0).

The section lies inside the frozen 65-parameter family, using just 15 free target parameters. Each C_i has at most nine nonzero entry positions (four diagonal, one upper, four lower), so the five matrices B have at most 40 nonzero positions altogether.

## 3. The coefficient-side bridge and global conclusion

The maps are

    X --phi--> W5 --pi2--> A^15,
    (A^15)_(a!=0) --sigma--> X.

On rings their composite is the standard injective localization

    R2 --pi2*--> A --phi*--> Q[X] --sigma*--> R2[a^-1].

If H in R2 has zero pullback to Q[X], its image under localization is zero. R2 is a polynomial domain and a is nonzero, so H=0 already in R2. This proves injectivity globally without assuming that the rational section extends across a=0. Equivalently the determinant projection contains the entire open set a!=0 and is dense in A^15. No claim about surjectivity on the boundary a=0 is necessary or made.

The identity K=I(closure(im phi)) is also elementary here: H composed with phi=0 iff H vanishes on im phi, and polynomial zero sets are closed, hence iff H vanishes on its closure. This independently reproves the precise B19-02 Lemma 3.1 bridge used in the statement.

## 4. Denominators and what has not descended

The only variable denominator in sigma is a; constants divide 8. Entries of C_i have denominators dividing 8a^2. The chosen B_i have no worse denominators. The section is a parametrization on an open set, not a purported coefficient equation or a normalization descent. Injectivity of R2 -> R2[a^-1] explicitly handles the omitted boundary. Neither normality of D45 nor descent from fibre-constant source functions is assumed. Indeed no source-only condition is being rebranded as an h: the coefficient-side kernel is proved to be zero.

## 5. Frame scope

D45 is stable under invertible linear changes of the five form variables: replace B_k by the corresponding linear combinations of the B's. Thus the same no-go holds for any single transformed copy of R2. It does not imply injectivity on the ring generated by two or more such copies, or on the GL5-span of U_d. Cancellations between different frames can lie outside the theorem. There is no claim of a representation decomposition or a multiplicity equality.
