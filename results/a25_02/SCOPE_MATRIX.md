## Derivation checks for S2 and S6

S2 (PROVED, hand check): lambda(t)=diag(t^-4,t,t,t,t) acts on a degree-s monomial by weight s-5 beta_1. For F=x_1 C all quartic input weights are <=-1. A degree-e equivariant output therefore has weights <=-e and beta_1>=ceil((s+e)/5). A positive-degree invariant has weight zero, so its value must vanish; conjugation handles any nonzero l and polynomial closure handles the rest. In the cubic convention, scalar substitution gives 4e=3+5k. Nonnegative partition entries force k>=0; k=0 gives no integral e, hence k>=1. The five-row constituent needs e>=5: iterating the Pieri rule on (Sym^4 V) tensor e times adds at most one row per factor, and Sym^e(Sym^4 V) is a submodule. Congruence e=2 mod 5 then gives e>=7. Thus l^2 divides the cubic, and det diag(l,l,m)=l^2m. No general covariant-to-determinantal-locus theorem is used.

S6 (PROVED as inherited factorization, checked by hand): source monomial block degrees (alpha,rho,kappa,sigma,nu) obey rho=kappa=d-alpha and sigma=2d+alpha-nu. With block weights (e_a,e_r,e_c,e_S,e_v), exponent=d beta+u alpha+h nu, beta=e_r+e_c+2e_S, u=e_a-e_r-e_c+e_S, h=e_v-e_S. The determinant configuration is A={(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2)}, whose convex hull is [0,1]x[0,2] and whose vertices all occur. C_arc removes nu>2d. Remaining degrees lie in d times that rectangle, so every forbidden interval component already occurs in C_arc. Exact d-fold support is {(p,q):0<=p<=d,0<=q<=2d,p<d or q even}. On alpha=d one has rho=kappa=0 and transposition is v -> -v, forcing even nu. Thus the support strengthening adds no rows either. No numerical five-vector rank or diagnostic sign is used.
