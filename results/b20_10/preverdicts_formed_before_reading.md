Claim as stated in the review: "The skew-degree projection commutes with the grading-preserving subgroup L, so b <= dim((S_lambda W)_{<0})^L".

Independent reasoning before reading the defence:
1. If the skew-degree grading on S_lambda W is induced functorially from a grading on W (eigenspaces of a cocharacter tau, or any direct-sum decomposition W = W_0 + W_1 with degree = count of W_1 factors), and L is BY DEFINITION the subgroup preserving each graded piece of W, then L preserves every graded piece of W^{(x)n}, hence of S_lambda W (a GL(W)-summand cut out by a Young symmetriser that commutes with the diagonal L-action and with the grading). So the projection pi_{<0} onto negative degree is L-equivariant. This step is FORMAL: it needs nothing beyond the definitions.
2. The non-trivial content is what b is. The bound b <= dim((S_lambda W)_{<0})^L is valid iff b = dim of a subspace of pi_{<0}(F) where F consists of L-invariant vectors, e.g. F = (S_lambda W)^G with L <= G, or F = the "forbidden subspace" defined as an L-stable object. If F is defined by a group H not contained in L (e.g. a stabiliser of a specific point, Stab(K5)) the bound FAILS to follow; if L is not contained in the group fixing F the bound fails.
3. A second risk: "grading-preserving subgroup" must mean the subgroup preserving EACH graded piece (centraliser of the cocharacter), not merely preserving the filtration (a parabolic). A parabolic P preserves the filtration by degree >= k and its invariants would NOT bound the projection dimension the same way.
4. A third risk: the projection of an L-invariant subspace F to degree <0 lands in ((S_lambda W)_{<0})^L, so dim pi_{<0}(F) <= dim((S_lambda W)_{<0})^L. That is only an upper bound on b; equality is NOT implied. The number dim((S_lambda W)_{<0})^L is a branching number (GL(W) -> L x C*) computed by a plethysm/character sum; any "count of triples" is a count of terms in that sum, and its correctness depends on the enumeration of the branching, which I cannot pre-verify without the definition.

Expected verdict: the commutation is PROVED as a formality provided L is the centraliser of the grading cocharacter AND b is the dimension of the projection of an L-stable space of L-invariants. The 10,505 count is an enumeration to be replayed, not a theorem.
