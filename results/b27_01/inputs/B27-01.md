## Why this slot exists

Batch 26 showed three facts about actual padding and the determinant closure `D`:
- Symmetric padding lies literally in `D` (A26-01).
- `lC ∉ D₄,₅` whenever `C` is a smooth cubic (C1, accepted).
- Pfaffian-square pencils such as `(A+B/2)²` and `Q²` are explicit determinants that defeat whole
  families of tableau tests (B26-02, A26-03).

No one has put these together into a map of the actual padding family.

## Ladder

**1a — The map.** Partition actual padding substitutions `T` (the 10×5 row data, i.e. `l` and the
nine entries of the 3×3 matrix `A`) into three classes:
- (i) `l·per₃(A) ∈ D` literally or in closure, by a committed certificate;
- (ii) `l·per₃(A) ∉ D₄,₅` by a committed certificate, for example smooth `per₃(A)` via C1;
- (iii) status unknown.

For each class, give its defining conditions on `T`, the certificate that places it there, and
its dimension, or a lower bound on it, inside the 50-parameter family. HAND or COMPUTED.

**1b — Smooth permanental cubics.** Is `per₃(A(x))` smooth for a general `A` with five-variable
linear entries? Answer with a certified explicit rational example, or a proof that it is always
singular. If a certified example exists, class (ii) is nonempty and gives an explicit
actual-padding point outside `D₄,₅`. Record it as `T*`, with a smoothness certificate: COMPUTED
(Jacobian ideal / Gröbner at a stated prime and over Q) or HAND.

**1c — Degree bounds at `T*`.** From committed record only, what degree window does the record
give for a determinant equation that is nonzero at `T*`? Examples: F2's `[6, 4^49]`, the cap-theorem
onset, Application 3's 245/299. State exactly which facts apply at `T*` and which do not. This rung
is bookkeeping; it does not search for an equation.

## Registered outcomes

For each rung: a proved or computed answer, a certified negative, or an exact obstruction. 1b's
positive outcome is **geometric noncontainment** at an explicit point. It is **not** a coefficient
equation. The binding constraint stands until someone exhibits an equation.
