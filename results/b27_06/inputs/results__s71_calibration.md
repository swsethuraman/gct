## What the calibration says about the route

* The **reversed** column order gave the largest cover at all 72 cells (the natural order second; fill orders and the random order well below).  The cover of `E` reaches `n_χ − a − excess` with excess 0 at 30 cells, ≤ 13 at 58 cells, and up to 220 (1.3 % of `n_χ`) on the `(…,8,1,1)`-type tails — larger than session 67's 0.1–0.6 % figure on some cells, so B4 is expected to be refuted; the residual is still two orders of magnitude below `n_χ`.
* The sieve on `E_red` certified `i_red = 0` outright at 8 of 72 cells (the rest went through the exact (★) rank on the exhibited kernel).
* **The hybrid phase itself is never the cost** (≤ 1.5 s at `n_χ = 22 000`): the build (`2.1·10⁻⁶ s` per `N_S·δ`) and the evaluation rows (`2.7·10⁻⁸ s` per point per `N_S·δ`, three families of `a + 8` points) dominate.  The slowest calibration cells are the `(4,4,4,4)` tail (`|Stab| = 24`, `N_S = 1.7·10⁶` at `n_χ = 17 896`): 22 s against session 60's 483 s on the same cell; `(16,7,3,3,3)_8`: 4 s against 507 s; `(13,13,8,1,1)_9`: 4 s against the sparse route's minutes.  C1 (prior `c_h` optimistic by ≥ 5×): **refuted** — the prior was pessimistic on the hybrid and silent on the evaluation rows.
