## 1. Why this table had to be rebuilt rather than inherited

`results/sixrow_census.md` carries `n_χ` by orbit enumeration only for its small
cells; every cell with `N_S > 3·10^6` or bound `> 40,000` carries the **bound**
`N_S/|Stab|`, marked `~`.  That bound is not a bound: orbits shorter than
`|Stab|` push the orbit count up, dropped orbits push `n_χ` down, and at the
balanced cells both happen.  Measured against the exact character count:

| λ | δ | census `~` | exact `n_χ` | error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(8,8,5,5,1,1)` | 7 | 75,474 | **62,613** | +21 % |
| `(8,8,7,3,1,1)` | 7 | 96,865 | **79,865** | +21 % |
| `(7,7,6,6,1,1)` | 7 | 104,066 | **87,045** | +20 % |
| `(8,8,6,4,1,1)` | 7 | 135,572 | **112,088** | +21 % |
| `(8,4,4,4,4,4)` | 7 | 83,836 | **92,031** | −9 % |
| `(8,8,6,2,2,2)` | 7 | 98,744 | **114,875** | −14 % |
| `(9,8,5,2,2,2)` | 7 | 158,009 | **182,806** | −14 % |

Wrong in both directions, by up to a fifth, at exactly the cells the balanced
sweep must order.  `analysis/wk9_s46_census.py` replaces it with

    n_χ = dim V_χ = (1/|Stab|) Σ_{g ∈ Stab} χ(g)·Fix(g),

`Fix(g)` the number of `g`-invariant weight-`λ` monomials, itself a small DP
over the `⟨g⟩`-orbits of the exponent set — no monomial enumerated, seconds per
cell.  It reproduces `N_S` and `n_χ` at all ten cells where both are known from
a measurement, exactly.

## 2. The cost model and its fit

At level `(s, 2)` the sampled and grouped stack has

    nnz_c = min( nnz , s · n_χ · (nnz/nrows) ) + K · n_χ,     K = a + 8,

the second term being the `K` pinned dense evaluation rows, and one sequence is
`4 · n_χ · nnz_c` element-operations.  Fitted over the ten measured cells:

- `nnz/nrows = 3.82` (measured range 2.9–4.6 over the 10 cells; this is the ratio the compression actually needs)
- `nrows/N_S = 0.88` (measured range 0.62–1.20 — much less stable, and only used where the level does *not* sample)
- `nnz/N_S = 3.36` (session 45 fitted 3.50 on nine cells; `(8,4,4,4,4,4)_7` came in at **2.06**, which is why this session does not use it)
- `ns per element-op = 0.491 + 2.853e-05·n_χ` (session 45 used a flat 2.3; the linear form is fitted to the 20 sequence records and is what the residuals below justify)
- `build seconds = 5.76e-05·N_S` (all three build stages together, generator reduction)

The ns-per-op residuals are the weak point, and they are not small: the two
`n_χ ≈ 10^5` cells straddle the fit by ±1.5 ns, so a predicted cost at
`n_χ ~ 3·10^5` — where the fit is extrapolating past every measurement — could
be out by a factor of two either way.  The table's ordering is robust to that;
its absolute hours are not.

## 4. What the table says

- **Balance 7 is now routine and balance 6 is affordable**: 11 of 33 balance-7
  cells and 5 of 18 balance-6 cells sit inside 48 h, and the four cheapest
  unmeasured cells in the whole band are all under 2 h each.
- **Balance 5 is the wall.**  Not one of the eight balance-5 cells fits in 48 h;
  the smallest has `n_χ = 476,063`, five times the largest cell ever measured.
  Session 45's table put the smallest balance-5 cell at 460,010 and one of the
  eight inside 48 h — with the exact `n_χ` and the refitted ns-per-op, none is.
- **Balance 4 is one cell wide and it is now measured.**  Of the three eligible
  balance-4 cells, `(8,4,4,4,4,4)` is the only one inside any plausible budget
  (`n_χ = 92,031`); the other two are at 897,481 and 1,752,488.
- **The most balanced weight of the degree is out of reach by ~30×.**
  `(6,6,4,4,4,4)_7`, balance **2**, `λ_1 < δ` so onset-only, `n_χ = 320,890`,
  `N_S = 14,564,331`: predicted 61 h and roughly 2.5 GB of build.  It is the
  natural first target of a successor with more cores, and it is the only
  six-row weight of degree 7 more balanced than `(8,4,4,4,4,4)`.
- **`h_pad` is in the table because it changes what a cell is worth.**
  `D > 0` requires `mult_pad > mult_det` and `mult_pad ≤ mult_red ≤ h_pad`
  (session 42, `docs/reducible_engine.md` §B), so a cell with `h_pad = 0`
  **cannot carry an obstruction whatever `mult_det` turns out to be**.  All
  three eligible balance-4 cells and all three onset-only cells have
  `h_pad = 0`.  Measuring them still decides the *determinant-ideal onset*,
  which is the open question; it does not decide the obstruction, which is
  already closed there.
