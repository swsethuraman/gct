# Session 52 — the `a = 1` census (`n = 4`, `r = 6`, `ℓ(λ) = 6`)

Region fixed in `results/PREREG_s52.md` §1 before any run: `λ ⊢ 4δ`, `ℓ(λ) = 6`
exactly, **obstruction-eligible** iff `λ_1 ≥ δ` (Corollary B of
`docs/reducible_ideal.md`; and, independently, Kadish–Landsberg's padding bound
at `(n,m) = (4,3)` — see `docs/bip_transfer.md` §3(a)).

**Routes.**  `a` by Frobenius plethysm (`wk8_s30_pleth.amb`) **and** by Weyl
alternation over a tail DP (`wk9_s42_census.a_weyl`), asserted equal at every
`a = 1` cell and at a random sample of the rest.  `h_pad` by Pieri strips over
the cubic plethysm (`wk9_s42_hpad.h_pad`) **and**, on a sample, by the Weyl
route (`wk9_s42_census.h_pad_weyl`).  `N_S` by the generating-function DP.
`n_χ ~` is the estimate `⌈N_S/|Stab|⌉`, which session 46 showed is **neither an
upper nor a lower bound** on the true `n_χ` (off by 21% in both directions on
different cells); the measured `n_χ` appears in `results/s52_ledger.md`.

**Lemma A** (`results/PREREG_s52.md` §2, proved before the census ran): at
`a = 1` the `h_pad` bound fires exactly when `h_pad = 0`, and `h_pad = 0` forces
`mult_red = 0`, hence `mult_pad = 0`, hence `i_pad = 1` and `D ≤ 0` — with no
measurement.  So the **informative** `a = 1` cells are exactly those with
`h_pad ≥ 1`, and the `h_pad = 0` cells are marked below and excluded from every
count of evidence.  (Session 47 was refuted partly for counting them.)
