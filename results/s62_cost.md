# Session 62 — cost curve

The single number session 63 needs is `|S|`, the support of the highest-weight vectors in
the **Foulkes basis** (block decompositions) — not the weight-space support `n_lam`.
`|S| = sum of orbit sizes over the monomials the HWVs touch`; the reduced block
`A = C_S^T beta_S C_S` costs `~ a*|S|^2 + a^2*|S|` (integrator note 3).

## The LMR cells — where `|S|` decides session 63

- **n = 4, delta = 24** (`(65,17,2^7)`, a = 274): `N_S = 156,438,903,314` (s57). `|S| <= N_S ~ 1.6e11`; the balanced-cell data (`|S| = N_S`) says it does not shrink materially. The reduced 273x273 block is trivial as a determinant; its **entries** cost `~273*|S|^2 ~ 3e24` — out of reach.

- **n = 3, delta = 12** (`(19,7,2^5)`, a = 6, the mandatory control): `|H_{3,12}| = 356,765,771,022,012,352,000,000` ~ 3.7e23, so the Foulkes Gram is out of reach **even for the control**. Its ground truth is therefore taken by the evaluation engine (`mult_det = a - nullity[E; ev_det]`), not the Foulkes Gram — the concrete proof that the Gram route cannot reach an LMR cell.


**Consequence for session 63.** The Foulkes/enumeration Gram cannot reach delta = 24. The reduced block route is bounded by `|S|`, and `|S|` at the LMR cell is ~1e11, so that route is out of reach too unless `|S|` collapses — which the balanced-cell measurements say it does not. Session 63 should report `|S|` at r = 9 as its first deliverable and treat the direct lambda-block (evaluation) route as the live one, exactly as the n = 3 control is done here.
